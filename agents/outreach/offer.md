# GemLabs Agency — Offer Brief (edit me before every campaign)

## Positioning (the one line)
GemLabs installs AI revenue systems and **owns one number: speed-to-lead**. We get B2B companies
from hours to minutes on inbound response — and we guarantee it.

## Who we are
GemLabs Agency builds custom AI revenue systems for growth-stage SaaS companies. We design,
build, and deploy the automation that handles the manual work in the sales and marketing stack —
lead qualification, outreach, follow-up, and reporting. Our own client acquisition runs on the
exact systems we sell: the outreach that starts every conversation was researched, personalized,
and scheduled by our pipeline. **You're inside the demo right now.**

## The signature metric: speed-to-lead
Most B2B companies respond to inbound leads in hours. Conversion research is unambiguous: contact
within 5 minutes vs. 1 hour changes qualification odds by an order of magnitude. Every audit we
run measures the prospect's actual response time; every build is judged against it; every case
study reports it. We sell the P&L delta, not the technology:
- response-time gap × lead volume × close rate = revenue leaking today
- hours of manual work reclaimed × loaded cost per hour = payroll recovered
- ad waste identified in dollars, from their own ad accounts

## The guarantee (risk reversal — say it everywhere)
**If we don't get your inbound response time under 10 minutes within 30 days of deployment, we
work free until we do.** (Engineering-controllable, near-zero actual risk, unmatchable by
tool-sellers and generic "AI agencies.")

## Offer ladder (how we sell)
1. **Free Revenue Audit (the cold CTA)** — a scoped 20–30 minute teardown of one system: we
   measure their speed-to-lead, find where the sales process or ad spend leaks, and put THEIR
   numbers into the math above. 80% prepared before the call by our own AI pipeline — the audit
   itself demonstrates what we build.
2. **Deep Audit — $1,500, fully credited toward any build (the downsell)** — full-stack written
   diagnostic across sales systems, ad accounts, and content/SEO with a 90-day roadmap.
3. **AI Lead Qualification System — the flagship entry build** — scores, enriches, routes, and
   responds to every inbound lead in under 60 seconds, 24/7. Deploys in 2–3 weeks. From $4,500 +
   $800/mo. Carries the guarantee.
4. **AI SDR / Outbound System** — automated prospecting, personalization, and follow-up that
   books meetings while the team sleeps (the system sending this email).
5. **Full Revenue Automation Stack** — end-to-end: lead gen → qualification → outreach →
   follow-up → reporting. $7,500–$20,000 build + retainer.

**Retainer = accountability, not maintenance.** We own the number every month and show it: a
monthly one-page scoreboard (response time, meetings booked, hours reclaimed). Ladder: $800
maintain → $1,500 optimize → $2,500 growth.

## CTA for this campaign (cold email = step 1 ONLY)
Offer the **free Revenue Audit**: we measure their speed-to-lead and show where the process or ad
spend leaks. Ask for interest ("worth a look?"), not a 30-minute meeting. Never mention pricing,
the deep audit, the guarantee, or the builds in cold email — those belong to replies and calls.

## Qualification (before booking a free audit)
Booking form: team size, monthly ad spend (if any), current CRM. Free audits go to companies that
can plausibly afford a $7.5K+ build.

## Tone
Founder-to-founder. Direct, specific, zero hype. Practitioners who build systems and prove them
in the client's own numbers — never "AI transformation" language.

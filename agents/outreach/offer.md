# GemLabs Agency — Offer Brief (edit me before every campaign)

## Who we are
GemLabs Agency builds custom AI revenue systems for growth-stage companies. We design, build, and
deploy automation that handles the manual work in the sales and marketing stack — lead generation,
qualification, outreach, follow-up, and reporting.

## Core outcome (lead with this)
Clients typically reclaim 15–20 hours of sales team time per week within the first 30 days, without
adding headcount.

## Offer ladder (how we sell)
1. **Free Revenue Audit (the cold CTA)** — a scoped 20–30 minute teardown of one system: where
   their sales process or ad spend is leaking, with a prioritized fix list. 80% prepared before
   the call by our own AI pipeline — the audit itself demonstrates what we build.
2. **Deep Audit — $1,500, fully credited toward any build (the downsell)** — full-stack diagnostic
   across sales systems, ad accounts, and content/SEO, delivered as a written report with a
   90-day implementation roadmap. Offered to prospects who are interested but not ready to build.
3. **AI Lead Qualification System** — scores, enriches, and routes every inbound lead in under 60
   seconds, 24/7. Deploys in 2–3 weeks. From $4,500 + $800/mo.
4. **AI SDR / Outbound System** — automated prospecting, personalization, and follow-up that books
   meetings while the team sleeps.
5. **Full Revenue Automation Stack** — end-to-end: lead gen → qualification → outreach → follow-up
   → reporting. $7,500–$20,000 build + retainer.

## Proof points (replace with real, current numbers before sending)
- Reduced lead response time from 4 hours to 8 minutes → 31% more booked demos (SaaS client)
- 20+ founders and RevOps teams served
- Most clients see measurable results in 30 days

## CTA for this campaign (cold email = step 1 ONLY)
Offer the **free Revenue Audit**: a short teardown of where their sales process or ad spend is
leaking and what to automate first. Ask for interest ("worth a look?"), not a 30-minute meeting.
Never mention pricing, the deep audit, or the builds in cold emails — those come up on the call
and in reply handling.

## Qualification (before booking a free audit)
Booking form asks: team size, monthly ad spend (if any), current CRM. Free audits go to companies
that can plausibly afford a $7.5K+ build; polite decline template for the rest.

## Tone
Founder-to-founder. Direct, specific, zero hype. We are practitioners who build systems, not a
marketing agency selling "AI transformation."

Operating Cadence — How We Stay on Track

Weekly (30 min, non-negotiable)

Reply rate by segment & hook · booking rate · no-show rate · inbox placement · pipeline value. Kill the worst angle, scale the best.

Monthly

Update SCALING-MODEL.md with actuals · re-forecast 3 months · price review vs. waitlist · authority scorecard (Clutch reviews, case studies, posts, citations).

Quarterly

GEO citation check on target queries · offer-ladder review (free vs. paid audit) · retainer churn autopsy · re-run of the 90-day no-reply recycling batch.

One metric per phase

M1–2: reply → booked audits. M3: close rate. M4+: delivery quality & churn. The moment fulfillment slips, proof stops compounding — cap intake, raise prices.
